## Description

Shopping on Zara or other foreign stores and shipping to Georgia? GE Parcel
Compare shows the full landed cost — transport, declaration fees, and the 18%
VAT that kicks in above 300 ₾ — directly on the product page, before you buy.

WHAT IT DOES
• Detects the product price and ships-from country automatically
• Compares Camex, Inex, USA2Georgia and Kiwi Post side by side, cheapest first
• Estimates weight by category (jeans, shoes, jackets…) — clearly labeled as
  an estimate, editable in one tap, and it remembers your corrections
• Accounts for volumetric weight (the shoe-box trap), per-carrier minimums,
  and 100 g rounding — the same math the forwarders use
• Flags the 300 ₾ duty-free threshold: see instantly whether the item clears
  customs free or adds 18% VAT
• Converts using the official National Bank of Georgia exchange rate

PRIVACY
Everything is computed in your browser. No accounts, no analytics, no data
transmitted anywhere — the only network requests are the official NBG
exchange rates. See the full privacy policy.

SUPPORTED
Zara (all EU stores, US, UK, Turkey) at launch; a generic detector works on
most other stores via the opt-in "any site" mode. Amazon and Trendyol are
coming next.

Rates are verified manually and dated in the UI. Estimates are estimates —
always check the forwarder's final invoice.
